## Final Presentation Rubric

Total: 100 points

| Criterion | Points | Full Credit | Partial Credit | Low / No Credit |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Overall organization and flow | 20 | The presentation is well organized and easy to follow. It has a clear beginning, middle, and end, and the major pieces of the project are connected into a coherent story. | The presentation is generally organized, but the flow is sometimes uneven or the connections between sections are not always clear. | The presentation is disorganized, difficult to follow, or lacks a clear structure. |
| Coverage of major project components | 25 | The presentation covers the major ideas from the full project and gives an appropriate overview of each part without getting lost in unnecessary detail. The most important results and conclusions are included. | Most of the major components are covered, but one or more parts are treated too briefly, unevenly, or with too much detail in the wrong places. | Several major components are missing, or the presentation does not reflect the full project. |
| Statistical correctness and interpretation | 25 | Statistical results are described accurately and interpreted correctly in context. The presentation shows a solid understanding of the methods used across the project and communicates the main conclusions clearly. | The presentation is mostly correct, but some interpretations are vague, incomplete, or only partly supported by the results. | There are major statistical errors, unclear interpretations, or conclusions that are not supported by the analysis. |
| Use of visuals and evidence | 20 | Visuals are clear, readable, and well chosen. They support the main points of the presentation and help explain the results rather than distracting from them. | Visuals are mostly clear and useful, but some are crowded, hard to read, or not fully integrated into the explanation. | Visuals are weak, difficult to read, missing, or not used effectively to support the presentation. |
| Delivery and pacing | 10 | The presentation is spoken clearly and stays within the time limit. The pacing is appropriate for a 7 to 10 minute talk, and the delivery is understandable and polished. | The delivery is understandable, but there are issues with pacing, clarity, or staying within the time limit. | The presentation is hard to hear or follow, significantly off pace, or does not fit the time limit well. |

### General Expectations

- The presentation should summarize the project clearly rather than retell every detail.
- The main emphasis should be on the most important results and conclusions from each part of the project.
- Claims should be supported by the analysis and visuals presented.
- Slides should be readable and should support, not replace, the explanation.
