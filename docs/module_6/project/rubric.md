### General Expectations

- Exploration beyond the required analysis is welcome, especially when the data raise interesting questions or reveal unexpected patterns.
- Work should be organized, readable, and complete.
- Parameter statements should be written in context, not just as symbols.
- Confidence intervals should be interpreted in terms of the population quantity being estimated.
- Assumptions should be discussed rather than merely stated.
- Students should show that the chosen procedure is appropriate for the data and the question being asked.
