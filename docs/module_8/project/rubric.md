### General Expectations

- Exploration beyond the required analysis is welcome, especially when the data raise interesting questions or reveal unexpected patterns.
- Work should be organized, readable, and complete.
- Notation should be explained in context, not just written as symbols.
- Students should distinguish between interpretation of the fitted line, a confidence interval, and a prediction interval.
- The linearity check should be based on the pattern in the data and used to judge whether SLR is reasonable.
- Interpretations of statistical output should be stated in terms of the variables in the project.
